Frame 5 · the differentiator
"Our signed data is tamper-evident — but a hash chain can't stop the operator rewriting history. So we freeze the chain head into an Arc receipt. This digest, computed live from our key registry, matches the one on-chain. Anyone can reproduce it — not even we can rewrite our own record."
