Predge on ArcProgrammable Money · Encode × Circle

The problem

The API economy is built for humans. The buyers are now machines.

  • Agents can't be human customers. No KYC, no credit card, no seat-based monthly plan. The signup funnel doesn't fit a wallet.
  • x402 fixed the request, not the settlement. Pay-per-call works — but on general chains USDC is "just another ERC-20": approvals, gas tokens, bridges, all for a $0.005 call.
  • Machine commerce needs one primitive, not a payment stack bolted onto a chain that was built for something else.

Why Arc

On Arc, the money is the chain.

msg.value

USDC is Arc's native token. The value an agent pays is the gas it spends — same asset, one step. No approvals, no bridges.

~60 lines

PredgeSettlement: payForRoute + a Paid event. Immutable, no upgradability, no custody in the service.

receipts

Payments are events — cheap, indexable, and verifiable by anyone. The receipt is the credential.

Arc collapses the machine-payment stack into a single call. That's the substrate Predge was waiting for.

Predge on Arc04

Track 2 · DeFi — Signal-Vault same repo

An agent-run USDC vault, steered only by signed signals.

  • Sense → verify → decide → act, with no human in the loop.
  • The keeper verifies Predge's ed25519 signature off-chain and commits the signal hash on-chain — so the vault's entire decision history is auditable.
  • Posture moves only on a verified signal. A signal it can't verify never moves funds.

FLAT → LONG

rebalance #1 — verified whale consensus turned risk-on.

LONG → SHORT

rebalance #2 — the signal flipped; the vault followed.

HELD

third poll, same signal → no tx. The agent doesn't churn gas when smart money hasn't changed its mind.

Predge on Arc05

The differentiator

Machine buyers don't take promises. They take receipts.

  • Predge's paid calls are signed + hash-chained — tamper-evident. But a hash chain alone can't stop the operator rewriting history wholesale.
  • anchor.mjs freezes the chain head into an Arc receipt — tamper-proof, externally anchored on a ledger we don't control. Not even we can rewrite our own audit trail.
  • Reproducible by anyone: curl … predge-keys.json | shasum -a 256 matches the digest sitting in the Arc transaction. --verify proves anchoring by scanning events.
